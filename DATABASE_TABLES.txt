NexAura - Database Tables (Current + Recommended)

Note:
- Types are shown in Django model style with SQL-equivalent hints in brackets.
- "Current" = already represented in your codebase.
- "Recommended" = needed to fully support appointments, advice chats, earnings, and dashboard metrics.

==================================================
CURRENT TABLES
==================================================

1) users_customuser  (Current)
--------------------------------
- id: BigAutoField [BIGINT PK]
- email: EmailField(unique=True) [VARCHAR(254) UNIQUE]
- username: CharField(max_length=150, null=True, blank=True) [VARCHAR(150)]
- role: CharField(max_length=50, choices=ADMIN|PATIENT|DOCTOR) [VARCHAR(50)]
- is_active: BooleanField(default=True) [BOOLEAN]
- is_staff: BooleanField(default=False) [BOOLEAN]
- is_superuser: BooleanField(default=False) [BOOLEAN]
- date_joined: DateTimeField(default=timezone.now) [TIMESTAMP]
- password: CharField (from AbstractBaseUser) [VARCHAR(128)]
- last_login: DateTimeField(null=True) [TIMESTAMP]

2) users_pendinguser  (Current)
--------------------------------
- id: BigAutoField [BIGINT PK]
- email: EmailField(unique=True) [VARCHAR(254) UNIQUE]
- otp_code: CharField(max_length=6) [VARCHAR(6)]
- password: CharField(max_length=128) [VARCHAR(128)]
- role: CharField(max_length=50) [VARCHAR(50)]
- profile_data: JSONField [JSON/JSONB]
- created_at: DateTimeField(auto_now_add=True) [TIMESTAMP]
- expires_at: DateTimeField [TIMESTAMP]

3) doctor_doctorprofile  (Current)
-----------------------------------
- id: BigAutoField [BIGINT PK]
- user: OneToOneField(users_customuser, related_name='doctor_profile') [BIGINT UNIQUE FK]
- specialization: CharField(max_length=100) [VARCHAR(100)]
- license_number: CharField(max_length=50) [VARCHAR(50)]
- phone: CharField(max_length=15) [VARCHAR(15)]
- full_name: CharField(max_length=255, default='Doctor') [VARCHAR(255)]
- preferred_name: CharField(max_length=100, default='Dr.') [VARCHAR(100)]
- nic_number: CharField(max_length=20, default='') [VARCHAR(20)]
- is_verified: BooleanField(default=False) [BOOLEAN]

4) patient_patientprofile  (Current)
------------------------------------
- id: BigAutoField [BIGINT PK]
- user: OneToOneField(users_customuser, related_name='patient_profile') [BIGINT UNIQUE FK]
- full_name: CharField(max_length=255) [VARCHAR(255)]
- date_of_birth: DateField [DATE]
- gender: CharField(max_length=10) [VARCHAR(10)]
- phone: CharField(max_length=15) [VARCHAR(15)]
- address: CharField(max_length=100) [VARCHAR(100)]
- medical_history: TextField(null=True, blank=True) [TEXT]

==================================================
RECOMMENDED TABLES (for your current frontend features)
==================================================

5) doctor_availability_slot  (Recommended)
-------------------------------------------
Purpose: Store doctor's online advice availability schedule.
- id: BigAutoField [BIGINT PK]
- doctor: ForeignKey(doctor_doctorprofile) [BIGINT FK]
- weekday: CharField(max_length=12)  # MONDAY...SUNDAY [VARCHAR(12)]
- start_time: TimeField [TIME]
- end_time: TimeField [TIME]
- is_active: BooleanField(default=True) [BOOLEAN]
- created_at: DateTimeField(auto_now_add=True) [TIMESTAMP]
- updated_at: DateTimeField(auto_now=True) [TIMESTAMP]

6) appointment  (Recommended)
-----------------------------
Purpose: In-person appointment records.
- id: BigAutoField [BIGINT PK]
- appointment_code: CharField(max_length=20, unique=True) [VARCHAR(20) UNIQUE]
- doctor: ForeignKey(doctor_doctorprofile) [BIGINT FK]
- patient: ForeignKey(patient_patientprofile) [BIGINT FK]
- appointment_type: CharField(max_length=20, default='IN_PERSON') [VARCHAR(20)]
- status: CharField(max_length=20)  # PENDING, CONFIRMED, CANCELLED, COMPLETED [VARCHAR(20)]
- scheduled_date: DateField [DATE]
- scheduled_time: TimeField [TIME]
- duration_minutes: PositiveIntegerField(default=30) [INTEGER]
- fee_lkr: DecimalField(max_digits=10, decimal_places=2) [DECIMAL(10,2)]
- notes: TextField(blank=True, default='') [TEXT]
- created_at: DateTimeField(auto_now_add=True) [TIMESTAMP]
- updated_at: DateTimeField(auto_now=True) [TIMESTAMP]

7) advice_chat_thread  (Recommended)
-------------------------------------
Purpose: Online advice chat conversation header between one doctor and one patient.
- id: BigAutoField [BIGINT PK]
- thread_code: CharField(max_length=20, unique=True) [VARCHAR(20) UNIQUE]
- doctor: ForeignKey(doctor_doctorprofile) [BIGINT FK]
- patient: ForeignKey(patient_patientprofile) [BIGINT FK]
- status: CharField(max_length=20)  # OPEN, CLOSED [VARCHAR(20)]
- started_at: DateTimeField(auto_now_add=True) [TIMESTAMP]
- last_message_at: DateTimeField(null=True, blank=True) [TIMESTAMP]

8) advice_chat_message  (Recommended)
--------------------------------------
Purpose: Individual messages inside an advice chat thread.
- id: BigAutoField [BIGINT PK]
- thread: ForeignKey(advice_chat_thread, related_name='messages') [BIGINT FK]
- sender_user: ForeignKey(users_customuser) [BIGINT FK]
- sender_role: CharField(max_length=20)  # DOCTOR or PATIENT [VARCHAR(20)]
- message_text: TextField [TEXT]
- is_read: BooleanField(default=False) [BOOLEAN]
- sent_at: DateTimeField(auto_now_add=True) [TIMESTAMP]

9) payment_transaction  (Recommended)
-------------------------------------
Purpose: Record payments for appointments and advice chats to calculate earnings.
- id: BigAutoField [BIGINT PK]
- transaction_ref: CharField(max_length=50, unique=True) [VARCHAR(50) UNIQUE]
- doctor: ForeignKey(doctor_doctorprofile) [BIGINT FK]
- patient: ForeignKey(patient_patientprofile) [BIGINT FK]
- appointment: ForeignKey(appointment, null=True, blank=True) [BIGINT FK]
- chat_thread: ForeignKey(advice_chat_thread, null=True, blank=True) [BIGINT FK]
- payment_for: CharField(max_length=20)  # IN_PERSON_APPOINTMENT, ADVICE_CHAT [VARCHAR(20)]
- amount_lkr: DecimalField(max_digits=10, decimal_places=2) [DECIMAL(10,2)]
- platform_fee_lkr: DecimalField(max_digits=10, decimal_places=2, default=0) [DECIMAL(10,2)]
- doctor_earning_lkr: DecimalField(max_digits=10, decimal_places=2) [DECIMAL(10,2)]
- status: CharField(max_length=20)  # PENDING, PAID, FAILED, REFUNDED [VARCHAR(20)]
- paid_at: DateTimeField(null=True, blank=True) [TIMESTAMP]
- created_at: DateTimeField(auto_now_add=True) [TIMESTAMP]

10) doctor_fee_config  (Recommended)
-------------------------------------
Purpose: Store current fee settings shown on doctor profile/dashboard.
- id: BigAutoField [BIGINT PK]
- doctor: OneToOneField(doctor_doctorprofile) [BIGINT UNIQUE FK]
- in_person_fee_lkr: DecimalField(max_digits=10, decimal_places=2, default=0) [DECIMAL(10,2)]
- advice_chat_fee_lkr: DecimalField(max_digits=10, decimal_places=2, default=0) [DECIMAL(10,2)]
- updated_at: DateTimeField(auto_now=True) [TIMESTAMP]

==================================================

- experience_years: PositiveIntegerField(default=0)
- location: CharField(max_length=120, blank=True, default='')
- qualifications: JSONField(default=list)
- hospitals: JSONField(default=list)
- languages: JSONField(default=list)
- availability_for_online_advice: BooleanField(default=False)

(Using separate tables is better for reporting and filtering at scale.)
